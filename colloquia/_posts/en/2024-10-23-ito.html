--- title: IMF transition across various environments in the early universe speaker: Ito Mana affil: Tohoku University webpage: "https://mana-ito-en.weebly.com/" date: 2024-10-23 time: "13:00" # Must use quotes place: Seminar Room A host: Hidenobu Yajima lang: en tags: [first stars, IMF, SPH] #pdf: img_thumb: colloquium-2024-10-23-thumb.jpg img: - colloquium-2024-10-23.jpg remarks: categories: - colloquia - en --- We study the thermal evolution of star-forming clouds in the primordial halos at $z>100$, considering the effect of the cosmic microwave background (CMB). The CMB inhibits the formation of H2 via the photodissociation of H-, resulting in a higher temperature than in the standard evolution of the first stars at $z<100$. At $z>500$, the cloud evolves almost isothermally at high temperature, and supermassive stars ($\sim 10^5 M_\odot$) are formed by direct collapse.
