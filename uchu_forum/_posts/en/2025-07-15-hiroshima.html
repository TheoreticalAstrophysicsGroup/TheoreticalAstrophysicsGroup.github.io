--- title: Dark matter signatures in/from the Universe speaker: Nagisa Hiroshima affil: Yokohama National University webpage: "https://soryushi.ynu.ac.jp/Nhirosima/index.html" date: 2025-07-15 time: "15:10" # Must use quotes place: Seminar Room A host: Yuka Kaneda lang: en tags: [dark matter, high-energy astrophysics, semi-analytic models] #pdf: img_thumb: dark-matter-web-thumb.jpg img: - dark-matter-web.jpg remarks: categories: - uchu_forum - en --- Dark matter (DM) is a mysterious energy component of our Universe which occupies about a quarter of its total energy density. The existence of DM is necessary for realizing our visible world, however, the detailed properties of DM are still unknown. In this talk, I will explain the motivation and possible strategies that we can take to extract information on the nature of DM, focusing on the so-called indirect searches and the structure formation.
